## Background

v0.1.6 delivered design/execution experience, independently assessed business goals, shared records and scoped network observation. v0.1.7 adds whole-scope resource clarification, first-run records, immutable task/rule revisions, whole-scenario status selection, full declared scope and improved report reading. These establish reusable facts; testing effectiveness and design quality still require controlled comparisons and review.

The audit identified concrete gaps. Selecting failed goals expands to their whole scenarios; users now want to rerun a specific minimum test point by its identifier. Candidate tasks still require review of dates, data and prerequisites. Retained browser scripts are execution fragments rather than complete reusable components. Actions sometimes share an entire evidence list, making the faulty step difficult to find. A goal remaining in violation does not prove the same defect persists. Full selection cannot find new requirements omitted from an outdated scope. Engineering checks, synthetic exercises and real trials have different evidence scopes; pass counts alone do not establish testing effectiveness.

This document summarizes those issues without copying private accounts, endpoints, run IDs, requirements or evidence. The [historical v0.1.7 preview](v0.1.7-preview.md) remains available.

## Requirements and acceptance direction

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R018-01 | Give every independently assessable test point a stable identifier, including verified, violating, inconclusive and untested points. Existing goals are the main mapping unit; actions are not automatically points. | Unique, readable, searchable and copyable IDs survive across runs. Content revisions and run/attempt IDs remain separate. Preserve case/goal mappings and explicit split/merge/retirement relationships; never recycle an ID for another goal. `SAMPLE-TP-000001` is only a synthetic example. Defect identifiers may coexist but cannot replace point IDs. |
| R018-02 | Rerun one or more point IDs while retaining their required setup, identities, paths, object relationships and readback. Track the original phenomenon separately from new findings. | Unknown or ambiguous IDs are not guessed. Point reruns, existing whole-scenario status reruns and full regression are explicit distinct scopes. Setup does not manufacture results for unselected goals. Status changes, defect recurrence, comparability and repair attribution remain separate evidence-based AI judgments. |
| R018-03 | Base full regression on an explicitly versioned, complete target inventory checked against current confirmed requirements. | New, changed, retired, unorganized, blocked, deferred and unassessed coverage stays visible. Do not filter full scope by prior outcome or maturity; distinguish requested scope from the executable subset. Impact checks state their basis and authorization. |
| R018-04 | Link actual actions, inputs, readback and evidence precisely at capture/record time. | A reader can reach the faulty step and supporting material directly. Shared evidence and missing step records are explicit; avoid copying every plan reference and file to every action. Retain original observations and history without inventing execution or duplicating content. |
| R018-05 | Separate replaceable data parameters from business expectations and bind actual values with current provenance. | Preserve ownership, role, history, version and dependency relationships. Record known deployment/model/host/Ego versions and initial state, leaving unavailable facts unknown. Changed data does not silently weaken the original rule. |
| R018-06 | Reuse stable, parameterized Ego operation fragments with explicit applicability and receipts. | AI selects and combines fragments. Unexpected state returns facts rather than automatic business retries, alternate routes, object substitution or verdicts. Retain intent, dispatch, result and reconciliation for side effects; reuse is not permission to resend. |
| R018-07 | Evaluate testing effectiveness and design quality under comparable conditions. | Use reviewable expectations, known defects and normal controls to assess coverage, useful findings, false positives/negatives, evidence, authorization and completion. Preserve improvements, regressions and variation. Review capability reuse, responsibilities, evidence reliability, necessary dependencies and maintainability without a fixed composite score or business route. |
| R018-08 | Observe download completion, obtain the actual file and check its business content. | Investigate and test relevant ordinary-link, new-page, transient-page, browser-generated and asynchronous exports on the installed version. Link the current UI action, download and file without confusing old or concurrent files. Distinguish completion, file access, readability and content correctness. Preserve required UI semantics and report observation gaps separately from product failures. Supported representative paths must complete the actual file/content chain; research alone is not delivery. |
| R018-09 | Show retest history and result comparisons within test reports. | Keep first-run reports free of empty history panels. Link initial, subsequent and current results with stable point IDs and source-specific evidence. Separate actual retests, assessment revisions, case changes, deployments and report regeneration; distinguish original-issue recurrence, new findings, outcomes and comparability. Preserve originals and exclude unselected historical points from current counts. Keep HTML/Markdown/JSON consistent. |
| R018-10 | Deliver visual case documents and pause for human review. | Generate HTML/Markdown from the same design asset with sources, IDs, expectations, dependencies, data needs, coverage and open questions. Provide navigation, search, filters and details. Preserve actual comments, revisions and explicit confirmation of version/scope before corresponding execution; trace the baseline to that confirmation. Re-review material changes; reuse unchanged confirmed cases for retests. Code handles records and checks, AI handles design/judgment. Design-only work creates no artificial execution run, and review confirmation is separate from operational authorization. |
| R018-11 | Improve requirement mapping, coverage and test design quality. | Carry forward relevant normal, boundary, exceptional, permission, state and dependency coverage. Trace requirements and revisions to independently assessable goals and necessary semantics; expose omissions and ambiguity without inventing expectations from UI or inflating case counts. Human review distinguishes structural validity from confirmed business coverage. |
| R018-12 | Review and maintain the applicability of design/execution experience. | Review stage, prerequisites and limits against actual misjudgments and recovery cases. Maintain useful generic guidance and counterexamples after human review; ordinary runs remain read-only. Record actual improvements and remaining failures rather than inferring quality from model upgrades or more guidance. |

R018-07 carries forward coverage, valid-defect, false-positive/false-negative, evidence, authorization and completion evaluation work, with design tradeoffs supported by facts and validation. Do not invent a complete defect population or a composite score. Engineering tests, synthetic exercises, real trials and human quality review are separate evidence scopes.
