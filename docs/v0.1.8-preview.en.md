# v0.1.8 preview: testing quality and test-point regression

[简体中文](v0.1.8-preview.md)

Status: requirements recorded on 2026-10-31 after the maintainer's local trials, report review and regression-design audit. Not implemented or released. v0.1.7 is finalized at its reviewed scope; see [release validation](v0.1.7-release-validation.md).

## Background

v0.1.6 delivered design/execution experience, independently assessed business goals, shared records and scoped network observation. v0.1.7 adds whole-scope resource clarification, first-run records, immutable task/rule revisions, whole-scenario status selection, full declared scope and improved report reading. These establish reusable facts, not a measured guarantee of quality or lower cost.

The audit identified concrete gaps. Selecting failed goals expands to their whole scenarios; users now want to rerun a specific minimum test point by its identifier. Candidate tasks still require review of dates, data and prerequisites. Retained browser scripts are execution fragments rather than complete reusable components. Actions sometimes share an entire evidence list, making the faulty step difficult to find. A goal remaining in violation does not prove the same defect persists. Full selection cannot find new requirements omitted from an outdated scope. Finally, the tools do not record model usage per run, so target-count ratios cannot establish token savings.

This document summarizes those issues without copying private accounts, endpoints, run IDs, requirements or evidence. The [historical v0.1.7 preview](v0.1.7-preview.md) remains available.

## Requirements and acceptance direction

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R018-01 | Give every independently assessable test point a stable identifier, including verified, violating, inconclusive and untested points. Existing goals are the main mapping unit; actions are not automatically points. | Unique, readable, searchable and copyable IDs survive across runs. Content revisions and run/attempt IDs remain separate. Preserve case/goal mappings and explicit split/merge/retirement relationships; never recycle an ID for another goal. `SAMPLE-TP-000001` is only a synthetic example. Defect identifiers may coexist but cannot replace point IDs. |
| R018-02 | Rerun one or more point IDs while retaining their required setup, identities, paths, object relationships and readback. Track the original phenomenon separately from new findings. | Unknown or ambiguous IDs are not guessed. Point reruns, existing whole-scenario status reruns and full regression are explicit distinct scopes. Setup does not manufacture results for unselected goals. Status changes, defect recurrence, comparability and repair attribution remain separate evidence-based AI judgments. |
| R018-03 | Base full regression on an explicitly versioned, complete target inventory checked against current confirmed requirements. | New, changed, retired, unorganized, blocked, deferred and unassessed coverage stays visible. Do not filter full scope by prior outcome or maturity; distinguish requested scope from the executable subset. Impact checks state their basis and authorization. |
| R018-04 | Link actual actions, inputs, readback and evidence precisely at capture/record time. | A reader can reach the faulty step and supporting material directly. Shared evidence and missing step records are explicit; avoid copying every plan reference and file to every action. Retain original observations and history without inventing execution or duplicating content. |
| R018-05 | Separate replaceable data parameters from business expectations and bind actual values with current provenance. | Preserve ownership, role, history, version and dependency relationships. Record known deployment/model/host/Ego versions and initial state, leaving unavailable facts unknown. Changed data does not silently weaken the original rule. |
| R018-06 | Reuse stable, parameterized Ego operation fragments with explicit applicability and receipts. | AI selects and combines fragments. Unexpected state returns facts rather than automatic business retries, alternate routes, object substitution or verdicts. Retain intent, dispatch, result and reconciliation for side effects; reuse is not permission to resend. |
| R018-07 | Record auditable cost and evaluate quality under comparable conditions. | Associate available time, tool calls and provider-reported usage with runs, points and preparation/execution/diagnosis/report phases. Shared costs remain separate when attribution is unavailable. Preserve provider definitions for input/output/cache/reasoning usage; mark unavailable values rather than estimating them. Separate testing-agent usage from the tested product's model usage and distinguish tokens from money or plan allowance. |

R018-07 also carries forward the earlier coverage, valid-defect, false-positive/false-negative, evidence, authorization, completion and cost evaluation work. Use reviewable expectations, known defects and normal controls; report regressions and variation. Do not invent a complete defect population, a composite score or a savings percentage. Engineering tests, synthetic exercises, real trials and human quality review are separate evidence scopes.

## Implementation and data boundaries

The Chinese document contains the detailed background and acceptance directions. This is requirements planning, not implementation approval. Identifier format, dependency representation, parameter bindings, historical mappings and usage integration require a concrete design review before development.

Software remains `0.1.7-rc.1 / report-step-evidence-1`, with main schema v8, regressionVersion 1 and networkVersion 1. Planning v0.1.8 changes none of these. Do not add legacy compatibility by default or overwrite historical tasks, IDs, runs, reports or evidence. Create explicitly sourced new assets and mappings when structures change.

AI continues to plan and judge, Ego performs authorized actions, and local tools handle deterministic records and checks. No merge, push, Tag/Release, installed-Skill update or distributable package is part of this local review.
